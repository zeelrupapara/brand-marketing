Let’s Talk

Based in Atlanta, Georgia. Working with businesses nationwide.

Haley Mae works with business owners and leadership teams on social media, website content, photography, messaging, and the details that shape a company’s public image.

We get to know your business, understand what makes it different, and help you share that with the people who matter.

Why clients work with us

Personal attention. Good communication. Work that gets done.

We can work with your employees, coordinate outside specialists, or help organize projects that have been sitting on your to-do list.

Our goal is simple: make your job easier and help your company present itself well.

  • We keep things personal. When you work with Haley Mae, you communicate directly with Haley. We take the time to learn your business and stay involved in the details.

  • You don’t need to have everything figured out before calling us. Some clients know exactly what they want. Others just know their marketing needs attention.

Our approach

First, we get to know you.

Before we start changing things, we want to understand your company.

We want to hear how you got started, meet the people behind the business, and learn what your customers appreciate most.

Then we look at what you’re already doing well and where we can help.

Maybe your website needs attention. Maybe you have a great team and plenty of good work to share, but nobody has the time to put it on social media. Or maybe you simply need someone who can keep everything organized and consistent.

Whatever the starting point, we build the work around your business.

Services

What we can help with.

Every engagement is tailored to the client. Outside development, photography, and production expenses are included only when specified in the agreed scope.

  • Brand Management

    We help make sure your company’s messaging, photographs, social media, and other marketing materials work well together. When something needs updating, we help get it done.

  • Social Media

    We create and manage organic content for LinkedIn and Instagram. That includes planning posts, writing copy, coordinating images, and sharing updates that give people a better look at your business.

  • Website Content & Coordination

    We help improve website writing, photography, organization, and overall presentation. If your site needs to be built or redesigned, we can work with a website specialist to manage the project.

  • Photography & Video

    We help plan and coordinate photography and video that show your business as it really is—your people, your workplace, your projects, and the work you’re proud of.

  • Executive Branding

    We work with owners and executives on professional biographies, photography, LinkedIn profiles, and other materials that help them introduce themselves and communicate with confidence.

  • Ongoing Brand Support

    As your business changes, we’ll help keep your information, messaging, images, and content up to date.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Haley Mae do?

We help businesses manage their brand presentation, social media, website content, photography, executive profiles, and related marketing projects.

Can you manage our social media?

Yes. We focus primarily on organic content for LinkedIn and Instagram. We’ll agree on a posting schedule and content plan based on what your business needs.

Can you help with our website?

Yes. We handle writing, messaging, photography coordination, and website presentation. We can also coordinate design and development through a website specialist when that work is included in the project.

Do you provide photography and video?

We plan and coordinate photography and video projects using qualified creative professionals. Production expenses are outlined before work begins.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Absolutely. We’re happy to work alongside your employees, designers, or other vendors.

Do you handle paid advertising or SEO?

Our primary focus is brand management, content, and organic social media. We can discuss involving outside specialists when other services are needed.

Do you work outside Georgia?

Yes. We’re based in the Atlanta area and work with clients across the country.

How do we get started?

Contact us and tell us a little about your business. We’ll arrange an introductory conversation to see how we can help.
