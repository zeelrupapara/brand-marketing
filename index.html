The opportunity

Your business should look as good online as it does in person.

You may have a strong team, loyal clients, and years of experience. But if someone visits your website or social pages for the first time, will they see that?

An outdated photograph, unclear message, or inconsistent profile can give the wrong impression. Haley Mae helps business owners close that gap—without trying to turn them into something they are not.

A different kind of partner

A close working relationship, not another layer of marketing.

Experience and perspective

Good brand work begins with knowing the people behind the business.

Every company has its own history, customers, and way of doing things. Before changing the way it presents itself, we take time to understand what makes it different. That is how the finished work starts to feel like the company itself—not a template anyone could use.

What better looks like

A more consistent, professional presence.

  • A website that explains what you do in plain language
  • Photography that shows your actual people and business
  • Social pages that look active, current, and true to your company
  • Clearer business and leadership profiles
  • Marketing materials that feel like they belong together
  • A dependable process for keeping everything up to date

You should feel comfortable sending someone to your website, showing them your LinkedIn page, or handing them a company presentation—because they all reflect the same business.

Where improvements often begin

Small changes can make a noticeable difference.

  • An outdated website

    Clearer writing, current photography, and information that is easier to find.

  • Inconsistent social media

    A recognizable look and a practical content plan your team can sustain.

  • Different messages in different places

    One straightforward description of who you are, what you do, and who you serve.

  • Leadership that is hard to find online

    Professional photographs, accurate biographies, and thoughtful visibility.

  • One-off marketing projects

    A more coordinated plan with someone keeping track of the details.

About Haley

Meet Haley Atenhan

Haley founded Haley Mae around a simple belief: the way a company presents itself should feel true to the people and the work behind it.

Her background includes brand presentation, communications, and client-facing work across different types of organizations. Those experiences taught her to pay attention to the small things—how people are introduced, how a message sounds, whether a photograph feels natural, and whether everything fits together.

Haley graduated summa cum laude from Liberty University with a degree in Social Sciences. Today, she works with business owners and leadership teams on brand messaging, digital content, photography direction, and the day-to-day details that shape a company’s public presence.

Frequently asked questions

What does Haley Mae do?

We help businesses manage how they present themselves online and in public. That can include brand messaging, organic social media, website content, professional photography coordination, executive profiles, and ongoing brand consistency.

Can Haley Mae help with a website?

Yes. We can help shape the writing, photography, structure, and overall presentation of a website. For design and development, we can coordinate with a website specialist when that work is included in an agreed project scope.

Do you manage LinkedIn and Instagram?

Yes. We help plan and manage organic content, with an emphasis on showing the real people, work, and experience behind the business. The platforms and posting schedule are agreed upon for each engagement.

Do you handle paid advertising or SEO?

Not as part of our standard brand-management services. Our core work is brand presentation, content, and organic communication. If a project requires other specialists, we can discuss that separately.

Will you work with our existing marketing team?

Absolutely. We can work alongside internal employees, leadership, outside designers, or existing vendors. Our role is to help the work feel connected and keep the details moving.

Are photography and video included in the monthly fee?

Creative planning and coordination may be part of an engagement. Actual photography, video, website development, and other outside production work are included only when specifically stated in the written scope or quote.

Do you work only with Atlanta businesses?

No. Haley Mae is based in the Atlanta area and can work with businesses in other parts of the country.

How do we get started?

Tell us a little about your company and what you would like to improve. We will review your inquiry and arrange an introductory conversation if the services appear to be a good fit.

Is Haley Mae right for you?

We may be a good fit if…

  • Your business has outgrown its current website or marketing materials.
  • You have plenty of real stories to tell but no consistent way to share them.
  • Your social media does not reflect the caliber of your team or work.
  • You want leadership to be more visible without sounding self-promotional.
  • You need someone who can see the whole picture and keep it organized.
